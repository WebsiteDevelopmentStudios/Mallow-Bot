𝗫𝗣 & 𝗟𝗘𝗩𝗘𝗟𝗦
• Added `/xp` and `-mb xp` to show your XP, level, and XP needed for the next level.
• Added `-mb level` as an alternative level lookup.
• Added `/leaderboard` and `-mb leaderboard` for the server top 10 XP earners.
• Eligible chat messages award 10–20 XP, with a 60-second per-user cooldown. Messages beginning with `-mb` do not award XP.

𝗠𝗢𝗩𝗜𝗘 𝗖𝗢𝗜𝗡𝗦 & 𝗚𝗔𝗠𝗕𝗟𝗜𝗡𝗚
• Added `/balance` and `-mb balance` to check your Mallow Coins.
• Added `/daily` and `-mb daily` to claim 100 Mallow Coins once every 24 hours. Both command styles share the same saved cooldown.
• Added `/gamble`, `/coinflip`, `/slots`, `/roulette`, `/dice`, and `/blackjack`, with matching `-mb` commands.
• Added `/work` and `-mb work` to earn Mallow Coins.
• Added `/pay` and `-mb pay` to transfer coins to another member.
• Added `/rob` and `-mb rob` to attempt to steal coins from another member.
• Mallow Coins are fictional server currency and have no real-world value.

𝗙𝗨𝗡 & 𝗦𝗢𝗖𝗜𝗔𝗟
• Added `/vibecheck` and `-mb vibecheck` for a random vibe score.
• Added `/ship` and `-mb ship` for a just-for-fun compatibility score between two members. Prefix syntax: `-mb ship [optional @member] @member`; if you give only one member, the bot pairs them with you.
• Added `/fortune` and `-mb fortune` for a random humorous fortune.
• Added `/wyr` and `-mb wyr` for a movie-themed Would You Rather prompt.

𝗧𝗘𝗫𝗧 𝗖𝗢𝗠𝗠𝗔𝗡𝗗𝗦
• Use `-mb help` or `-mb commands` to see the command list.
• The help list includes XP, gambling, utility, social, music, and movie commands.
• Added `-mb uptime`, `-mb update`, `-mb commands`, and `-mb sync`.
• Bot mentions now reply with a randomized short self-description from `description.txt), avoiding immediate repetition.
• `/say` has no `-mb say` equivalent; its invocation is private and the bot message is public.

𝗠𝗨𝗦𝗜𝗖 & 𝗩𝗢𝗜𝗖𝗘

𝗠𝗢𝗩𝗜𝗘 𝗖𝗢𝗠𝗠𝗔𝗡𝗗𝗦
• Added `-mb movie list` to list local movie files.
• Added `-mb movie search <title>` to search titles and show provider page links.
• Added `-mb movie play <URL>` to post a movie page link in the configured channel.
