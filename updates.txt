Mallow Bot — New in This Update

- Added the per-server swear counter toggle in the dashboard.
- Added `-mb swears` to view your own counted messages in the current server.
- Added `-mb swear-leaderboard` to show the server's top ten swear-counter totals.
- Added an owner-only developer panel to add and remove words from the global swear list.
- Swear counting is passive: it counts a message once when it contains a configured word and does not delete messages or apply punishments.
