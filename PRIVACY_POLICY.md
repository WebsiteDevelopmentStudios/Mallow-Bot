# Mochi Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026

Mochi Bot (“Mochi,” “we,” “us”) is a Discord bot that provides community features such as commands, XP and levels, leaderboards, virtual Mochi Coins, games, and other server utilities. This policy explains what information the bot may process when you use it.

## 1. Information the bot may process

Depending on the features enabled and how you interact with Mochi Bot, it may process or store:

- Discord user, server, and channel IDs needed to respond to commands and keep server-specific data separate.
- Usernames or display names and message/command content that you send to the bot, where needed to respond or process a feature.
- Feature data such as XP, levels, leaderboard values, virtual currency balances, daily-claim timestamps, game results, and settings.
- Operational information such as errors and logs needed to run, secure, and troubleshoot the bot.
- Information sent to the bot through optional integrations, such as a movie or media lookup, when you request that feature.

The exact data depends on which features are active. Do not send passwords, payment card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information to the bot.

## 2. How information is used

Information may be used to:
- Provide commands and requested features.
- Maintain XP, leaderboards, virtual currency, cooldowns, and game state.
- Prevent abuse, enforce cooldowns, troubleshoot errors, and maintain service reliability.
- Respond to support requests and improve the bot.

Mochi Coins are fictional in-bot points. They are not real money, have no cash value, and cannot be redeemed for money through the bot.

## 3. Storage and sharing

Bot data may be stored in the bot’s configured hosting, database, or file storage. The project may use third-party infrastructure and APIs to host the bot or fulfill requested features. Those providers process data according to their own policies.

We do not intend to sell personal information. We do not share user data for advertising purposes. Data may be disclosed if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the bot, its users, or its infrastructure.

## 4. Messages and third-party services

The bot may read messages or message content only to the extent enabled by its Discord permissions and intents, including to recognize commands, respond to mentions, or process supported link features. Discord itself processes your use of Discord under Discord’s own policies.

When a command requests an external service, relevant query or link information may be sent to that service. Check the provider’s privacy policy for its practices.

## 5. Retention and deletion requests

Data is retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant features, maintain records, and troubleshoot or protect the service. Retention periods may vary by data type and hosting configuration.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of bot-stored data, contact the bot operator through the support or contact method listed where Mochi Bot is distributed. Include your Discord user ID and the relevant server or feature so the request can be located. Do not include passwords or other secrets. We may need to retain some records for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Removing the bot from a server may stop future processing for that server, but it may not automatically erase previously stored records.

## 6. Children

Mochi Bot is a community tool on Discord and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users must comply with Discord’s minimum-age requirements and applicable local law.

## 7. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect stored data, but no online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features, hosting, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published with an updated effective date. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use, to the extent permitted by law.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Mochi Bot operator through the official support or contact channel associated with the bot.
