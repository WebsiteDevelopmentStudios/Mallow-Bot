Mallow Bot — New in This Update

- Added a per-server swear counter toggle to the dashboard.
- Added `-mb swears` to check your own swear-counted messages in the current server.
- Added `-mb swear-leaderboard` to show the top ten members by count in the current server.
- Added owner-only swear-word management to the developer panel, including adding and removing words.
- The counter counts each matching message once and does not delete messages or apply punishments.
