# General Halloween facts, selected once per day without repeats.
Pumpkins became the most common jack-o'-lantern material in North America because they are native to the region and easy to carve.
The word Halloween comes from “All Hallows' Eve,” the evening before All Saints' Day.
Trick-or-treating became a widespread Halloween custom in the United States during the twentieth century.
The tradition of carving lanterns was associated with turnip-like root vegetables before pumpkins became popular in North America.
Black cats became associated with Halloween through folklore and superstition in parts of Europe.
Many modern Halloween traditions combine older harvest-season customs with local community celebrations.
